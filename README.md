A frontier LLM reading club open to PhDs, industry professionals, students, or anyone curious about the LLM world.

We're a community exploring the foundations of large language models through rigorous discussion — meeting to engage with influential papers, exchange ideas, and learn together.

## 👥 Attendees: 234

## 📅 Logistics

- **Cadence:** Biweekly
- **Location (in-person):** Vancouver Downtown
- **Location (online):** Microsoft Teams (link provided in invitation)
- **Calendar:** Follow our [Luma calendar](https://luma.com/calendar/cal-FKFCQcjSPzJvCiC) for upcoming sessions and invites

## 🙋 For Participants

Read the paper beforehand if you can — but don't let it stop you from joining if you didn't fully get through it or understand it. The group is primarily for discussion, so come with questions and curiosity either way.
